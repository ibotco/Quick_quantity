# Quick Quantity — descriptions

## One line

An offline Bill of Quantities generator that turns a picture or PDF of a drawing into a priced, editable bill.

## Short (≈60 words)

**Quick Quantity** turns a photo, scan or PDF of a drawing into a priced Bill of Quantities. Upload the set, set the scale, and it measures the plan and writes the bill, the material and labour schedules and the cost breakdown — all in one offline `index.html` file, with PDF, Word, Excel and CSV exports. Nothing is uploaded; it works with no connection.

## Standard (≈150 words)

**Quick Quantity** is an offline Bill of Quantities generator for builders, contractors and quantity surveyors.

Upload a picture or PDF of a floor plan — or the whole drawing set: site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, schedules and services layouts — and the app reads the sheet, measures it and writes a priced Bill of Quantities. It decides what the building is from the words printed on the drawing — house, office or shop, clinic, church, school block — and loads that building's room schedule, specification and rates rather than a default.

Every figure stays editable: correct a quantity or a rate on the take-off sheet and the bill, the schedules, the cost breakdown and the exports all follow. The finished bill goes out as PDF, Word, Excel or CSV, laid out A4 for printing.

It is a single `index.html` file with no server, no installation and no internet connection — the drawing is read on the device and never uploaded, so it works on site, on a phone.

## Notes for a listing

- **Offline first** — one file, no server, no account, no network; the plan never leaves the device.
- **Reads the set** — a PDF's own text layer, the drawing itself, and the schedules on it; a Generate button reads all of them at once.
- **Measured, then priced** — quantities are computed from the geometry, with checks that catch a plan read wrongly.
- **Editable throughout** — every quantity, rate, room and opening can be corrected; totals follow live.
- **Real documents** — PDF, Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) written inside the browser, plus CSV and A4 print.
- **Kept** — bills are saved inside the app and a project file can be reopened later.
- **Not tied to one market** — rates ship with Ghanaian practice and can be swapped for a price feed or your own.
